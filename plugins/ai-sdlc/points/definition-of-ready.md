A ticket is ready to hand to an agent when:
- it says what must be true once the work is done, not only what is wrong;
- it has acceptance criteria that can be checked: an expected value, an example, an observable behaviour;
- it says where it happens (a file, a module, a screen) or what is out of scope;
- it leaves no decision open ("to be defined", "to check with…");
- it fits in one reviewable change. Otherwise it is split, and each piece has its own criteria.
